## The project

LEICHT is a German kitchen maker. I worked on its digital products through caisy, a headless CMS company.

## What I built

- **The leicht.com brand site,** end to end, in Qwik, with content from a headless CMS over GraphQL and pages in several languages. It includes the navigation and its search, the dealer search, the partner appointment-request flow, the hero and project sliders, and the catalog order form.
- **An internal kitchen configurator,** hosted on AWS. It is an internal tool, so there is no public page to link to.

## What it shows

Building features for a large brand in several languages on a content system that editors use. That is the work behind [building your web app or website](/services/full-stack-development/) and [Arabic and bilingual web apps](/services/arabic-rtl-web-apps/).
