## The project

LEICHT is a German kitchen maker. I worked on its digital products through caisy, a headless CMS company, as part of a team.

## What I built

- **The multi-language kitchen configurator,** built on a headless CMS and GraphQL. The configurator is an internal tool, so there is no public page to link to.
- **Parts of the leicht.com brand site,** built in Qwik with GraphQL content: the navigation, the dealer search, the partner appointment-request flow, the hero and project sliders and the catalog order form.

## What I didn't build

The site had a lead engineer who built most of the rest of the code. I worked as part of a team, not alone.

## What it shows

Building features for a large brand in several languages on a content system that editors use, and working inside a shared codebase with other developers.
