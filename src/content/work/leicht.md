## The project

LEICHT is a German kitchen maker. I worked on its digital products through caisy, a headless CMS company.

## What I built

- **The multi-language kitchen configurator,** built on a headless CMS and GraphQL. The configurator is an internal tool, so there is no public page to link to.
- **Parts of the leicht.com brand site,** built in Qwik with GraphQL content: the navigation, the dealer search, the partner appointment-request flow, the hero and project sliders and the catalog order form.

## What it shows

Building features for a large brand in several languages on a content system that editors use. That is the work behind [building your web app or website](/services/full-stack-development/) and [Arabic and bilingual web apps](/services/arabic-rtl-web-apps/).
