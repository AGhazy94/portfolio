## Is this for you?

This fits if you want AI to do a specific job inside a product, and you need someone to build the feature and everything around it:

- You run a product and want a chat interface, an assistant or an agent that works with your own data.
- You have an idea for an AI product and need a first version that real users can try.
- You've seen a demo that works and need it to work for many users, with accounts, limits and a bill you can predict.
- Your team has the model part sorted and needs someone to build the web app and background jobs around it.

It isn't a good fit if the work is training a model, or heavy research.

## What you get

**A narrow first version.** We start by choosing one job the AI must do well, and how you will know it did. Most AI projects go wrong by starting too broad.

**The AI part and the product part together.** The interface, the call to the model, the tools it can use, the database and the sign-in. One person who holds the whole picture.

**Background work that doesn't freeze the screen.** Long tasks run in the background and report progress to the user, so a slow model call doesn't block the page.

**Visibility and limits.** Logs of what was asked and answered, error reporting, and caps so a bug can't produce a surprise bill.

**Notes you can hand on.** How the feature works, what it costs per use and what to watch.

## What the first two weeks look like

1. **Week one:** the job, the success test, a list of example inputs and the outputs you'd accept, and the product skeleton on a live address.
2. **Week two:** the first working version of the feature, behind a sign-in, tried against your examples, with logging turned on.

After that we improve it using real examples from real use.
