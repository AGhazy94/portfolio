## Is this for you?

This fits if one of these sounds familiar:

- The developer who built your app stopped replying, or left, and nobody else knows how it runs.
- You have a working product with real users and a backlog that keeps growing.
- An agency handed over a repository and you can't tell whether it is in good shape.
- You have one in-house developer and need a second pair of hands who can start without a long handover.

It is not a good fit if you want a brand-new product built from nothing. [MVP development](/services/mvp-development/) covers that.

## What you get

**A short audit first.** I read the code, the deployment setup and the data model. You get a plain-language note that says how the app is built, what is solid, what is risky and what I would change first. No score, no jargon.

**Access in your name.** Repository, hosting, domain and third-party accounts end up under your ownership, with me added as a collaborator.

**Small, reviewable changes.** Bug fixes and features go in as pull requests. You can read each one, or have someone read it for you.

**Notes as I go.** What the app does, how to run it, how to deploy it and what I changed. If I disappear tomorrow, the next developer can start.

## What the first two weeks look like

1. **Week one:** access, a local setup, the audit and a short call to go through it together. We agree what to fix first.
2. **Week two:** the first fixes and the first small feature, shipped to production if the deployment is safe to use, or to a staging copy if it isn't.

After that we settle into whatever rhythm suits you: a set number of days a month, or work by the task.

## Experience inside other people's codebases

I haven't only built things from scratch. Since 2022 most of my work has been in codebases that other people set up:

- At caisy, a headless CMS company, I built features on Next.js, tRPC and Drizzle codebases: schema and migrations for Stripe billing and subscriptions, and AI translation of content fields in the editor.
- On ScriptBee's Next.js app I joined a team's repository and built the chat interface, background tasks, the Google Ads connection and the onboarding flow. The [case study](/work/scriptbee/) lists what was mine and what wasn't.
- At Smart Bricks I moved data fetching to the Next.js App Router and built the Storybook component library inside an existing product.
