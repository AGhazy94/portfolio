## Is this for you?

This fits if you are a founder or a small team with an idea, and you need a first version that real people can use, not a demo. It fits especially if:

- You have no technical co-founder and want one person you can talk to directly.
- You have a budget and a date, and you want a clear list of what is and isn't in version one.
- You'd like the code to stay useful when you hire your first engineer.

It is not a good fit if you already have a large product that needs a team. [Taking over an existing app](/services/take-over-an-app/) is closer.

## What you get

**A smaller scope than you expect.** The first call is mostly about cutting. We list what the product must prove, and anything else moves to a "later" list.

**One developer from first commit to launch.** Design translation, front end, back end, database, authentication, deployment. The same person makes the decisions and knows the code.

**Plain, common tools.** TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js and PostgreSQL. Boring on purpose: they are easy to hire for and easy to hand on.

**A live link early.** I deploy from the first week so you see the product grow and can react while changes are cheap.

**Handover notes.** How to run it, how to deploy it, where the secrets live and what I'd do next.

## What the first two weeks look like

1. **Week one:** scope, a clickable outline of the main flow, the repository and hosting set up in your accounts, and the data model agreed.
2. **Week two:** sign-in and the first real feature, deployed, so you can click through it on a live address.

From there we ship in small steps, and each step is a version you can show to someone.

## Relevant experience

At caisy I built features for billing and subscriptions (schema, migrations and Stripe-related work) inside a Next.js codebase. On ScriptBee I built a chat interface, a task widget and background tasks that run through Trigger.dev. Those are the pieces most MVPs need: accounts, payments, something running in the background and a clean interface. The [case study](/work/scriptbee/) lists exactly what was mine.

## How we work and what it costs

Small MVPs are usually best as a fixed price for a fixed scope, split into milestones you approve one by one. If the scope is still moving, a monthly arrangement is safer for both of us. I quote after one call, and the quote lists what is included and what isn't.
